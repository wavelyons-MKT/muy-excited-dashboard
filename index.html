Which topics do best

How the typical (median) episode on each topic scores compared with 100, a typical episode overall. Topics with at least 2 scored episodes. Topic tags are a first draft, still to be reviewed by the team, and with this few episodes the differences are a first hint, not a pattern.

Sources. Podcast downloads and estimated unique listeners from Omny Studio (both Muy Excited feeds). Instagram and Facebook from Meta's Graph API, for posts on Futuro Media's accounts that mention Muy Excited. Followers are Futuro Media's, shared by all Futuro shows.

Score uses each episode's downloads in its first 30 days; episodes need 4 comparable episodes to get a score. Projections for newer episodes are based on how earlier Muy Excited episodes built up, so they will get more reliable as the show adds episodes.
